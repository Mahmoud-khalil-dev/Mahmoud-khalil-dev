## What I do

I lead a Flutter team at Azzrk and own the multi-tenant white-label platform behind its commerce product line — one codebase that generates fully branded client storefronts through build-time configuration injection rather than per-client forks. I own the release pipeline for it end to end, as the single escalation point across hundreds of App Store and Google Play submissions.

Outside the platform work, I ship products where Flutter alone is not enough: network-layer content filtering written natively against `NetworkExtension` and `VpnService`, and a live telemedicine app with real-time chat, voice and video that I built and published as sole developer for a Dubai healthcare provider.

## Problems I have actually solved

**Filtering traffic without touching other apps.** Rqeab blocks content, tracking and malicious advertising at the network layer. That meant writing a tunnel provider natively on both platforms and driving it from Dart across platform channels, then defending the design through App Store review — an entitlement class Apple scrutinises closely.

**Making one codebase behave like hundreds of apps.** Per-tenant identity is injected at build time through flavors and `--dart-define`, so a new client storefront is a configuration change, not a branch. The hard part was never the theming; it was bundle identifiers, certificates, provisioning profiles, entitlements and store metadata staying coherent across 500+ builds.

**Getting medical software through review.** Lounge Clinic handles patient-supplied photos, video, contact and location data. Shipping it meant App Privacy declarations, a 16+ medical content rating, and written justification for camera, microphone and location access.

**Turning rejections into a process.** I wrote the submission playbook my team now works from, and average rejection remediation time dropped 20–30%.
